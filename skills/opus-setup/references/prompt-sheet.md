# Prompt sheet template

Write `opus-prompts.md` in this shape. Fill the three prompts from the person's own jobs. Keep the checklist; keep the supplier example only if it fits their work, otherwise drop it.

```markdown
# My Opus 5.5 prompts

## Before you hit send
- Have I said what I want back?
- Have I given it the information it needs?
- Have I said what finished looks like?

## 1. <Job name>
<One ready-to-copy prompt: the whole job in one message, the real files or details to use, and what to hand back, include, check and flag. Placeholders in <angle brackets> for anything to swap in.>

## 2. <Job name>
<Same shape.>

## 3. <Job name>
<Same shape.>

## Example: comparing supplier quotes
Compare the three supplier quotes in this project folder against our requirements and budget. Include delivery, installation and ongoing costs. Create a comparison spreadsheet and a one-page recommendation. Check the totals and flag anything the quotes don't tell us.

Review the files, figures and recommendations before you use them.
```

A good prompt from this sheet, for a "monthly client report" job:

> Create this month's report for <client> from the files in <folder>. Include revenue against last month, the three biggest changes and anything that needs their attention. Finish with a one-paragraph summary I can paste into an email. Check every figure against the source files and flag anything that looks off.
